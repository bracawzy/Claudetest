How it works

If variable costs are 60% of your price, each sale leaves you a 40% margin. A 20% discount cuts that margin in half, to 20%, so you need to sell twice as many units (+100%) just to make the same profit.

The formula: more units = discount ÷ (margin − discount), where margin = 100% − variable costs. If the discount is as big as the margin, every sale loses money and no amount of extra sales can make up for it.

Based on the break-even example in Hermann Simon's Power Pricing.
